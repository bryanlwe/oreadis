About

I'm Bryan, based in Sydney, Australia. This is where I write up the trips that I have taken — backpacking and hiking across Asia and Oceania. Each post covers how to get there, where to stay, what to eat, hikes/walks worth doing, and what it cost. No sponsored content, no luxury options.

Oreadis comes from oread, the mountain nymphs of Greek mythology. Roughly translated, Oreadis means "of the mountain". It felt fitting for a site mostly built around hiking, trails, and time spent outdoors.
